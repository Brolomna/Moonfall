Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Moonfall is a werewolf party game played in one room. One person hosts the game on their phone and everyone else joins from their own phone. This page explains what information the game uses, where it is kept, and how ads work.

No accounts

You don't sign up or log in to play. We don't ask for your email address, phone number, real name, location or any payment details.

What the game uses while you play

  • The name you type when you join. Everyone in the same game sees it, and so does the host.
  • Your role and what happens to you in the game (for example, whether you are still alive). Only you see your own role on your phone. The host sees everyone's role to run the game. Players who join after the cards are dealt watch the game and can see the roles.
  • Secret votes and night answers sent to or from your phone. The host only sees vote totals, not who voted which way.

This game information is kept in the game server's memory only while the game is running. It is not stored in a database, and it is gone when the server restarts.

What is saved on your own phone

Moonfall saves a few small settings in your browser's storage so the game works smoothly. They stay on your device and you can delete them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

  • A random player ID, so your phone can rejoin the same seat if the connection drops.
  • Your language choice and whether sound is on.
  • Whether you have already seen a message from the host.
  • On the host's phone only: the host PIN, a copy of the current game (so it can be restored if the server restarts), and the Hall of Fame (past winners). These never leave the host's phone except to restore a game on the same server.

Advertising

Players' screens may show a banner ad from Google AdSense. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information such as your device type, browser, IP address and the pages you visit.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Other services

  • Hosting. The game runs on a hosting provider that may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses and request times) for security and to keep the service running.
  • Instagram. The "Join our group" button opens Instagram. What happens there is covered by Instagram's own privacy policy.
  • Fonts. The game loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts load.

Children

Moonfall is a social party game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
